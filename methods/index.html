Two kinds of geography

Census tribal areas

Community indicators use the Cherokee OTSA (Oklahoma Tribal Statistical Area) and the White Earth Reservation with its off-reservation trust land. They count every resident, whether or not they are a Tribal citizen, and miss citizens living elsewhere.

Reference counties

Economic and energy indicators use the counties that overlap each Nation: 14 in Oklahoma and 3 in Minnesota. County totals describe the regional economy. They are not Tribal employment, Tribal businesses or Tribal energy assets.

Uncertainty

ACS values are survey estimates. Each comes with a 90% margin of error, shown as ± values and as shaded bands in charts. Margins for derived shares were approximated using Census Bureau formulas for ratios and proportions.

A 5-year estimate pools five years of responses, so consecutive estimates share four years of data. Treat movement between adjacent periods with caution, and compare non-overlapping periods (for example 2014–2018 with 2020–2024) before calling something a trend.

Small populations produce wide margins. White Earth estimates are less precise than Cherokee estimates for this reason.

What is not shown, and why

Federal funding totals are withheld. The pipeline searched USAspending.gov by recipient name. Those matches have not been verified, cover many years at once, and include contracts and assistance that may not reach either Nation. Publishing the totals would invite people to quote them as Tribal funding, so they stay in the private research repository until they can be verified.

No facility-level data. Load, outage, tariff, property condition and household-level information require authorization from each Nation and were not collected.

No targets or rankings. Targets, owners and priorities belong to each Nation and are left blank on purpose.

How this site is built

The analysis pipeline lives in a private repository. It downloads and checks each source, builds a set of canonical tables, and runs validation tests. A single export script then copies the public-safe subset into this site's data folder, so every figure here traces back to a pipeline table.

Charts are drawn in the browser with D3 from those exported files. Nothing is typed into the pages by hand, so refreshing the data updates every number at once.
