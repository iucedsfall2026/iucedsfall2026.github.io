Where the data comes from

The White Earth Reservation covers about 2,800 km² of land, including all of Mahnomen County and parts of Becker and Clearwater counties, a landscape of lakes, wetlands, forest and farmland. Small population counts mean wider margins of error.

The outlined area is the Census tribal geography used for community indicators. The shaded counties are the reference region used for jobs, businesses and energy.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025, simplified for display. Boundaries are statistical, not legal or ownership lines.
