Cherokee Nation

Conditions in the Cherokee Nation reservation area of northeastern Oklahoma, with a regional economy traced across 14 overlapping counties.

Community indicators: Cherokee OTSA (Oklahoma Tribal Statistical Area), all residents. Regional indicators: Adair, Cherokee, Craig, Delaware, Mayes, McIntosh, Muskogee, Nowata, Ottawa, Rogers, Sequoyah, Tulsa, Wagoner and Washington counties.

Where the data comes from

The Cherokee OTSA covers about 17,300 km² of land across all or part of 14 counties, including part of the Tulsa metropolitan area. Because Tulsa County is so large, regional totals lean heavily on it.

The outlined area is the Census tribal geography used for community indicators. The shaded counties are the reference region used for jobs, businesses and energy.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025, simplified for display. Boundaries are statistical, not legal or ownership lines.
