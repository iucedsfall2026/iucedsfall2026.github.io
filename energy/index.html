Energy and infrastructure

The physical context around both Nations: where electricity is generated, what is being built, which counties face the most hazard risk, and how well homes are connected.

County figures cover the 14 Cherokee reference counties in Oklahoma and the 3 White Earth reference counties in Minnesota. A plant located in a county is not evidence of Tribal ownership or of power available to the Nation.
