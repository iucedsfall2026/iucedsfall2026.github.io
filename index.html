Before you read the numbers

Census areas are not citizenship. Tribal figures describe everyone living in the Census tribal area, not enrolled citizens, and Nation members living elsewhere are not counted.

Counties give regional context. Jobs, businesses and energy figures cover reference counties that overlap each Nation. They describe the regional economy, not Tribal enterprises.

Survey estimates carry error. ACS values are 5-year estimates with 90% margins of error. Overlapping periods share data, so small year-to-year moves are not trends.

Nothing here is a decision. Proposed actions and scenarios show methods. Priorities, targets and approvals belong to each Nation.
